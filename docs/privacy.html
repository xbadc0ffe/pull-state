Pull State

Privacy Policy

Last updated May 2, 2026

Pull State is built to keep your shots private. Everything you log stays on your device.

Local-only storage

Pull State stores all of your data — shots, beans, equipment, and settings — locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. No data is transmitted to any server, third party, or external service.

No account, no tracking

You don't need an account to use Pull State. The app contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

Photos

Photos you attach to shots, beans, or equipment are stored on-device inside the app's sandbox using Apple's external storage mechanism. They never leave your device. Pull State only accesses the camera or photo library when you explicitly choose to add a photo.

Tip jar purchase

Pull State offers an optional tip jar purchase through StoreKit 2. The transaction is handled entirely by Apple — Pull State never receives or stores any payment information.

Device protection

Your data is protected by iOS file protection and remains within the app's sandbox.
